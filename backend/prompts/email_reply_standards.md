## Email reply standards (mandatory)

All email replies must follow these rules:

1. **Concise** — Prefer short replies (about 80–120 words). Longer only when the customer clearly asked for detail, specs tables, or a full quotation explanation.
2. **Specific** — Address their actual question, request, or next step. Reference a concrete point from their message when useful.
3. **Inquiry-related** — Stay on the topic of this customer’s inquiry. Do not pad with generic company history, full product catalogs, or unrelated certifications.
4. **No unsolicited essays** — Avoid long explanations, multi-paragraph product primers, or marketing fluff unless they specifically requested that detail.
5. **Still professional** — Warm and clear; plain text; no invented prices, MOQs, or commitments.
